Your manual FNA works. You do it on paper. Clients buy products. The app should do EXACTLY what your paper does — but faster, more impressive, and with AI detecting things you might miss.

**Your paper FNA has 5 pages. The app has 8 tabs because some pages split into input vs output.**

### 📄 PAGE 1 — DISCOVERY & GOALS

#### What you do on paper:
- Write down client name, age, spouse, kids
- Ask about goals: Growth, Income, No Risk/No Loss, Taxes, Accessibility, Legacy
- Capture: Time Horizon, Risk Tolerance, Suitability, Affordability
- Note down: 401(k) details (10%, pre-tax, growth), employer match, spouse retirement status
- Check off: Term insurance (1M/30Y), Mortgage, Brokerage, Roth IRA, Pension (0), College (0), Real Estate
- Note long-term care consideration

#### What the app does (Tab 1: Financial Background):
**Same questions, digital form.** 6 sub-sections:
1. **Employment & Income** — Client + spouse jobs, salaries, other income
2. **Retirement Accounts** — 401(k) details (balance, contribution %, match, loan), Roth, IRA, HSA, pension
3. **Investments & Assets** — Brokerage, stocks/RSUs, crypto, real estate, property in India
4. **Real Estate** — Primary home value + mortgage, rental properties
5. **Debts** — Credit cards, HELOC, personal loans, 401(k) loans, informal/hand loans
6. **Monthly Expenses** — Housing, utilities, food, education, insurance premiums, everything

**What you can't do on paper that the app does:**
- Health Score updates LIVE as you enter data (sidebar gauge: 42/100)
- Net worth calculates automatically as you enter assets and debts
- Monthly surplus/deficit shows immediately after income and expenses
